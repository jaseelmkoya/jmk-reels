## Science notes
- Real SCMaglev coils are figure-8 "null-flux" coils in the guideway side walls; the coils do not visibly glow — C5's glow is a visual metaphor.
- The train runs on rubber wheels below ~150 km/h; floats ~10 cm above. Keep the gap small in C1/C3.
- Levitation is by induced currents (Faraday + Lenz), not diamagnetism.
