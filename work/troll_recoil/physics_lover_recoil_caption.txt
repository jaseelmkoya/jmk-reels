Question (📝 PYQ: Plus One March 2023): While firing a bullet the gun recoils. Which conservation law explains this?
Answer: Law of conservation of momentum! (LCM അല്ല 😅)
⚖️ mv + MV = 0 → V = −mv/M — gun-ന്റെ mass വലുതായതുകൊണ്ട് recoil speed bullet-നേക്കാൾ വളരെ കുറവ്.
