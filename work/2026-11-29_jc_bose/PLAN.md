## Verified facts (sources)
- Born 30 Nov 1858, Mymensingh (now Bangladesh); died 23 Nov 1937. (ETHW biography)
- 1895, Presidency College, Calcutta: transmitted and received EM waves at 60 GHz over 23 m through two intervening walls; remotely rang a bell and detonated gunpowder. Components he built: spark transmitter, coherer, dielectric lens, polarizer, horn antenna. IEEE Milestone dedicated 15 Sep 2012. (ETHW IEEE Milestone page)
  - Popular accounts say Kolkata Town Hall, 75 ft (= 23 m), Lt-Governor present — not confirmed by IEEE, so NOT used.
- Wavelengths 2.5 cm to 5 mm. Check: λ = c/f = 3×10⁸ / 60×10⁹ = 5 mm ✓
- Demonstrated wireless ~2 years before Marconi (1897 system). Bose believed knowledge should be free; did not commercialise/patent his radio work (later patented only the galena detector). Telegraph-company owner offered to patent and share profits before his Royal Institution lecture — he refused. (ThePrint; ETHW)
- Marconi 1901 transatlantic receiver / Bose's mercury coherer: contested history → say only "പലരും പറയുന്നു" or leave out. LEFT OUT.
- Textbook (Plus Two Ch 8): microwaves → RADAR, satellite communication; EM waves need no medium; c = 3×10⁸ m/s. Exam favourite: uses of EM waves.
