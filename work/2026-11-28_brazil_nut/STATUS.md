- Why chain: gravity should pull the heavier nut down → but the jar is shaken → each shake opens a small gap under the big nut → small grains slip in, the big nut can't → it lands a step higher each time (ratchet / percolation) → plus granular convection: grains up in the middle, down in a thin wall layer too narrow for the big nut → it stays on top.
